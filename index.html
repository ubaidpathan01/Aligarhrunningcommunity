Frequently Asked Questions

Kya registration ka koi charge hai?

Nahi, Aligarh Running Community 100% free and non-profit initiative hai. Hamara maksad Aligarh ke logon ko healthy & active banana hai.

Agar main pehle kabhi nahi dauda toh?

Bilkul fikar na karein! Hamare paas "Beginners Walk & Jog" group hai jisme log aaram se walk aur jogging mix karke enjoy karte hain.

Assembly location kya rehti hai?

Aamtaur par meeting point AMU Circle / Riding Club area ya Shamshad market side rehta hai. Register karne par Sunday se pehle WhatsApp par exact pin location bhej di jati hai.

Kya females ke liye safe aur inclusive hai?

Haan bilkul, ARC ek bahut respectful, safe aur family-friendly community hai jisme students, working professionals aur seniors sabhi participate karte hain.
